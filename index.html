Our Mission

Riverbend Yoga Studio helps people make time for movement, breathing, and relaxation. We welcome beginners, busy professionals, and older adults. You do not need to be flexible or experienced to join a class. Our instructors offer options so students can practice at a comfortable pace.

Featured Class: Gentle Yoga

Gentle Yoga is a welcoming class for people who want to build confidence, improve mobility, and reduce stress. The instructor explains each movement and offers easier options when needed.
