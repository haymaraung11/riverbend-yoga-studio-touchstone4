Our Teaching Approach

Our goal is to make yoga feel approachable. Students are welcome to ask questions, take breaks, or choose a gentler option during class.

Accessibility and Accommodations

Students may tell us about mobility needs, injuries, sensory preferences, or other accommodation requests before class. We aim to provide clear verbal guidance, pose alternatives, and a welcoming environment. Please contact us in advance so we can discuss what support is available.

Studio Policies

  • Please arrive a few minutes before class begins.
  • Wear comfortable clothing and bring water.
  • Tell the instructor if you need a modification or a break.
  • If you need to cancel, contact the studio as early as possible.
  • Respect other students' space and help maintain a calm environment.
