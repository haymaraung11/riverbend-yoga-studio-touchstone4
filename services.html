Class Types

Gentle Yoga

A slower class focused on comfortable movement, balance, breathing, and flexibility. It is a good option for beginners and people who prefer a relaxed pace.

Vinyasa Yoga

A flowing practice that connects movement with breath. Instructors offer modifications, and students are encouraged to rest whenever they need to.

Restorative Yoga

A calming class using supported poses and quiet breathing to encourage rest and relaxation.

Private Sessions

Private sessions provide one-to-one guidance based on your goals and comfort level. Contact us to discuss availability and any accommodations you may need.

Pricing Overview

We offer single-class visits, class packages, and private sessions. Prices depend on the class type and package selected. Please contact the studio for the current price list before booking. There is no online checkout on this website.
